Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 1, 2025  ·  Last updated: May 1, 2025

Ireta is a private app for you and your closest people. We collect only what we need to make the app work, we never sell your data, and your content stays inside your tribe.

1. Who We Are

Ireta is an independent iOS application developed and operated by the Ireta Development Team ("we," "us," or "our"). If you have questions about this policy, contact us at iretahelp@yahoo.com.

2. What Information We Collect

Account information

Ireta uses Sign in with Apple for authentication. When you sign in, Apple shares a unique identifier and, at your option, your name and email address. We store the Apple-provided user ID to identify your account.

Content you create

When you use Ireta, we store the content you and your tribe create, including:

  • Tribe names and invite codes
  • Profile nicknames, taglines, and profile pictures
  • Book pages including text and photos
  • Skills and ratings
  • Comments, polls, events, bulletins, and Peanut Gallery slides
  • Activity feed entries

Usage data

We use Firebase by Google for our backend. Firebase may collect basic usage diagnostics such as crash reports to help us fix bugs. We do not use this data for advertising.

Device information

We do not collect your device identifier, location, contacts, or sensor data.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account
  • To display your content to other members of your tribe
  • To send push notifications about activity in your tribes
  • To improve app stability using crash reports
  • To respond to support requests

We do not use your information for advertising, build advertising profiles, or sell your data to any third party.

4. Who Can See Your Content

Your content is private to your tribe. Only members with the correct invite code can see that tribe's content. We do not make any tribe content publicly visible.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Google LLC) — authentication, database, file storage, and crash reporting. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Apple Sign in with Apple — authentication. Apple Privacy Policy

We do not integrate any advertising networks or data brokers.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. If you request deletion, we will remove your personal information within 30 days. Deleted content may persist in database backups for up to 90 days before being purged.

7. Children's Privacy

Ireta is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used Ireta, contact us at iretahelp@yahoo.com.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data. To exercise any of these rights contact us at iretahelp@yahoo.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Security

We use encrypted data transmission, Firebase security rules, and Apple's authentication infrastructure to protect your information.

10. Changes to This Policy

When we make changes we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of Ireta after a policy change means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Reach the Ireta Development Team at iretahelp@yahoo.com.
