Privacy Policy

Effective May 1, 2025  ·  Last updated May 1, 2025

Ireta is a private app for you and your closest people. We collect only what we need to make the app work, we never sell your data, and your content stays inside your tribe.

1. Who We Are

Ireta is an independent iOS application developed and operated by the Clem Tech ("we," "us," or "our"). Questions? iretahelp@yahoo.com

2. What Information We Collect

Account information

Ireta supports Sign in with Apple and email/password authentication. We store a unique user ID and, optionally, your email to identify your account.

Content you create

  • Tribe names, invite codes, and member lists
  • Profile nicknames, taglines, and photos
  • Book pages including text and photos
  • Skills, ratings, votes, and points balances
  • Comments, polls, rankings, favorites, events, bulletins, and Peanut Gallery slides

Purchase data

Point packs and plan purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive confirmation of successful purchases and store your points balance. We never see or store your payment card information.

Usage data

We use Firebase (Google) for our backend, which may collect basic crash reports to help us fix bugs. We do not use this data for advertising.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account
  • To display your content to members of your tribe
  • To manage your points balance and tribe plan access
  • To send push notifications about tribe activity (only if you grant permission)
  • To improve app stability and respond to support requests

We never use your data for advertising, build advertising profiles, or sell your data to any third party.

4. Who Can See Your Content

Your content is private to your tribe. Only members with the correct invite code can access that tribe's content. Invite codes expire after 30 days for your security. We never make tribe content publicly visible.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Google LLC) — database, file storage, authentication, crash reporting. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Apple Inc. — Sign in with Apple authentication and in-app purchase processing. Apple Privacy Policy

We do not integrate any advertising networks, data brokers, or analytics platforms beyond the above.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained while your account exists. If you request deletion we will remove your personal data within 30 days. Deleted content may persist in backups for up to 90 days before being fully purged.

7. Children's Privacy

Ireta is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Contact us at iretahelp@yahoo.com if you believe a child under 13 has used Ireta.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data. Email iretahelp@yahoo.com and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Security

We use encrypted data transmission, Firebase security rules, Apple authentication, and expiring invite codes to protect your information and your tribe.

10. Changes to This Policy

When we make significant changes we will notify you through the app. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

11. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Reach the Clem Tech at iretahelp@yahoo.com.
