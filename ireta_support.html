Support

Need help with Ireta? Browse common questions below or reach out directly — we respond within 24 hours.

Getting Started

How do I create a tribe?

After signing in tap the + button on the group selection screen. Give your tribe a name and you'll get a 10-character invite code to share with your people.

How do I join a tribe?

Tap "Join a tribe" on the group selection screen and enter the 10-character invite code. Codes expire after 30 days — ask your leader for a new one if yours isn't working.

How do I sign in?

Ireta supports Sign in with Apple (no password needed) and email/password sign-in. Both options appear on the login screen.

Points & Pricing

What are points?

Points are Ireta's one-time currency. Buy them once and they never expire. You spend points each month to keep your tribe's plan active — there are no auto-renewing subscriptions.

How do I buy points?

In the app go to Group Settings → Plan → Upgrade. You can buy a point pack (100, 500, 1,200, or 5,000 points) or a plan bundle that includes bonus points alongside tribe access.

What happens if my points run out?

If your balance drops below the monthly point cost your tribe returns to the Free tier. All existing content is preserved — you just lose access to paid features until you top up your points again.

Can I use points across multiple tribes?

Yes — your point balance is tied to your account, not a single tribe. You can use points to run multiple tribes at different tiers from the same balance.

What does the Free tier include?

Free tribes support up to 7 members, 500 MB storage, 3 bulletins, 1 ranking, 1 book (3 pages max), and Peanut Gallery for the leader only. Polls, meetups, and favorites require a paid plan.

What's the difference between plans?

All paid plans unlock unlimited bulletins, rankings, books, pages, polls, meetups, favorites, and all-member Peanut Gallery. Tiers differ on member count, storage, and PDF export (Legacy and above only).

The Archives

Who can create books and add pages?

By default any member can. The group leader can restrict this in Archive settings — tap the gear icon in The Archives tab.

Can I add photos to a page?

Yes — tap the photo icon when editing to add images from your camera roll. Photos are compressed automatically.

Can I edit a page after saving?

Yes. Open the page and tap the edit button to update the text, title, or photos.

The Peanut Gallery

How do I create my slide?

Go to The Peanut Gallery tab and tap +. Add a title, caption, and optional photo. Your slide is visible to everyone in the tribe.

Can I comment on my own slide?

No — you can see what the tribe says but you can't comment on your own slide. Keeps it honest.

Who can post a slide on the Free tier?

On the Free tier only the group leader can add a Peanut Gallery slide. All members can add slides on any paid plan.

Town Square

How do I create a meetup?

Town Square → Meet-ups → tap +. Add a title, date, location, and description. Meetups require a paid plan.

Polls, Meetups, or Favorites aren't showing?

These features require a paid plan (Starter or above). Upgrade in Group Settings → Plan to unlock them.

How many bulletins can I post?

Free tribes can post up to 3 bulletins. Paid plans get unlimited bulletins.

Account & Privacy

Who can see my tribe's content?

Only members of your tribe. All content is private and invite-only. We never share your data outside your circle.

How do invite codes work?

Codes are 10 characters and expire after 30 days. Leaders can rotate the code early from Group Settings if needed.

How do I leave a tribe?

Group Settings → scroll to the bottom → "Leave the Circle." You can rejoin anytime with the invite code.

How do I delete my account?

Email iretahelp@yahoo.com with the subject "Delete my account" and we'll remove your data within 30 days.

How do I sign out?

Any tribe → settings gear → scroll to the bottom → Sign Out.
