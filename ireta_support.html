Getting Started

Points & Pricing

Points are Ireta's one-time currency that power your tribe's plan. When you purchase points they are added directly and automatically to your tribe — no setup required. Points never expire and there are no auto-renewing subscriptions.

In the app go to Group Settings → Plan → Upgrade. Choose a point pack or a plan bundle. The moment your purchase completes, the points are automatically credited to your tribe and the plan upgrades instantly.

If your balance drops below the monthly point cost your tribe returns to the Free tier. All existing content is preserved — you just lose access to paid features until you top up your points again.

Points are purchased directly for a specific tribe — when you buy a point pack or plan, you select which tribe receives them. Each tribe has its own point balance, so you can upgrade multiple tribes independently.

Free tribes support up to 7 members, 500 MB storage, 3 bulletins, 1 ranking, 1 book (3 pages max), and Peanut Gallery for the leader only. Polls, meetups, and favorites require a paid plan.

All paid plans unlock unlimited bulletins, rankings, books, pages, polls, meetups, favorites, and all-member Peanut Gallery. Tiers differ on member count, storage, and PDF export (Legacy and above only).

Town Square

Town Square has six sections — Rankings (rank each other in any category), The Verdict (agree/disagree debates), Scoreboard (track game and task scores), Bulletin (post to your tribe), Chirp (quick one-tap alerts to the whole tribe), and Meet-ups (plan hangouts with location and RSVP). All features except Bulletin are on paid plans.

Town Square → Meet-ups → tap +. Add a title, date, location, and description. The creator can edit it after posting and the tribe gets notified of changes. Meetups require a paid plan.

These features require a paid plan (Starter or above). Upgrade in Group Settings → Plan to unlock them.

Yes — bulletins support both photos and video attachments. Tap the media icon when composing a bulletin to add one.

Free tribes can post up to 3 bulletins. Paid plans get unlimited bulletins.

The group leader sets categories (Best Cook, Worst Driver, Most Likely to Be Late, etc.) and everyone votes on who holds each title. Results are visible to the whole tribe and can be archived to your lore permanently.

Account & Privacy

Only members of your tribe. All content is private and invite-only. We never share your data with anyone outside your circle.

Group leaders can customise the tribe's theme (colours and mood), endonym (what the group calls itself — Crew, Squad, Fam, etc.), sigil (the group's icon), and permissions for who can create books, add pages, and share the invite code. All settings are in Group Settings → the gear icon.

Codes are 10 characters and expire after 30 days. Leaders can rotate the code early from Group Settings if they think it's been shared with the wrong person.

Group Settings → scroll to the bottom → "Leave the Circle." You can rejoin anytime with the invite code.

Email iretahelp@yahoo.com with the subject "Delete my account" and we'll remove your data within 30 days.

Any tribe → settings gear → scroll to the bottom → Sign Out.
