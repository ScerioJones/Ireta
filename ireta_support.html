Support

Need help with Ireta? Browse the common questions below or reach out directly — the Ireta Development Team responds within 24 hours.

Getting Started

How do I create a tribe?

After signing in tap the + button on the group selection screen. Give your tribe a name and you'll get a six-letter invite code to share with your people.

How do I join a tribe?

On the group selection screen tap "Join a tribe" and enter the six-letter invite code. You'll be added instantly.

How do I sign in?

Ireta uses Sign in with Apple — no password needed. Just tap the sign in button and use your Apple ID.

The Archives

Who can create books and add pages?

By default any member can create books and add pages. The group leader can change this in Archive settings — tap the gear icon in the top right.

Can I add photos to a page?

Yes — when editing a page tap the photo icon to add images from your camera roll.

Can I edit a page after saving?

Yes. Open the page and tap the edit button to update the text, title, or photos.

The Peanut Gallery

How do I create my slide?

Go to The Peanut Gallery tab and tap +. Add a title, caption, and optional photo.

Can I comment on my own slide?

No — you can see what others say but you can't comment on your own slide. Keeps it honest.

How do skill ratings work?

List up to 5 skills and rate yourself on a half-star scale from 0.5 to 5.

Town Square

How do I create a meetup?

Go to Town Square → Meet-ups → tap +. Add a title, date, location, and description. The creator can edit it after posting and the tribe gets notified of changes.

How do I post to the Bulletin?

In Town Square tap Bulletin then "Post something." Up to 500 characters. Anyone in the tribe can comment or react.

What are Rankings and Favorites?

Rankings let the leader set categories and everyone votes. Favorites let everyone rate things the tribe loves on a 1–5 scale.

Account & Privacy

Who can see my tribe's content?

Only members of your tribe. All content is private and invite-only. We never share your data with anyone outside your circle.

How do I leave a tribe?

Go to tribe settings → scroll to the bottom → tap "Leave the Circle." You can rejoin anytime with the invite code.

How do I delete my account?

Email us at iretahelp@yahoo.com with the subject "Delete my account" and we'll remove your data within 30 days.

How do I sign out?

Go to any tribe → settings gear → scroll to the bottom → tap Sign Out.
