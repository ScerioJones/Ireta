Social media was built for the public.
Ireta was built for your people.

The big platforms want you performing for strangers. Ireta gives you a private space to be real with the people who already know you.

  • No public profiles — your tribe is invisible to the outside world
  • No algorithm deciding what people see or when
  • No ads, no data selling, no tracking
  • Leader-controlled access — you decide who gets in
  • Invite codes expire after 30 days for your security
  • Multiple tribes — your family never sees your friend group
🛡️

Your story stays yours

Private by design. Invite-only by default. Your lore is never visible to anyone outside your tribe.

Pricing

Pay when you need it.
No subscriptions ever.

Ireta runs on points — a one-time currency you buy when you want and spend when you're ready. No auto-renewing charges. No surprises.

🪙

Buy points once

Purchase a point pack or a plan bundle. Points are yours to keep — they never expire.

🔓

Unlock your tribe

Choose a tier and duration. Points are deducted monthly to keep your tribe's features active.

🫂

Everyone benefits

Every member of your tribe gets full access to your plan's features — they pay nothing.
